  - 'Tricycle franchise owners and tricycle owners'

  - 'Filled-up application form for MTOP (walk-in at BPLO)'
  - 'Cedula/CTC (get from the barangay hall where an applicant resides or in the Municipal Treasurer)'
  - 'Barangay Clearance (get from the barangay hall where the route is located)'
  - 'Route Endorsement (from the Route President)'
  - 'Updated Official Receipt and Certificate'

steps:
  - "Fill out the necessary details and submit the accomplished application form. | The office would tell the applicant to proceed to Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) for payment. The said office is just facing the specific BPLO window for tricycle permit applications."
  - "Proceed to the Municipal Treasurer's Office to pay corresponding fees. | The breakdown of fees: Mayor's Permit costs P200, occupational fee at P150, health certificate at P85, sticker at P20, and addition of P25 for a penalty."
  - "Claim the mayor's permit, franchise, and the sticker."
