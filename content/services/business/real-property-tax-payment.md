fees: 'Varies'
fee_details:
- "Dependent on the real property's assessed value (AV); basic tax and special education fund (SEF) is at 1% of the AV each."
time: '~12 mins'
office: 'Office of the Municipal Treasurer'
requirements:
  - 'Copy of Tax Declaration (from Municipal Assessor's Office)'
  - 'Copy of latest tax receipts (from the payee/client)'
whocanavail:
  - Real property tax owners or their representatives
steps:
  - 'Proceed to the Office of the Municipal Treasurer and present the requirements or provide tax declaration numbers.'
  - 'The concerned agency will check or verify the last real property tax payments, and compute the tax if preceding payments are completely made.'
  - 'Pay the corresponding amount and receive the issued official receipt.'
postscripts:
  - '* covers basic taxes only; for individuals, CTC costs ₱1 for every ₱1,000 gross income plus the ₱5 basic tax; for corporations/groups, CTC costs ₱2 for every ₱5,000 annual gross income and assessed value of propoerty plus the ₱500 basic tax.
