  - 'Filled out application form for business permit'
  - 'Location sketch/picture of the business location'
  - 'DTI registration certificate'
  - 'Barangay clearance'
  - 'Cedula/CTC'
  - 'Zoning certificate/locational clearance (does not apply to those in ICC/IMFP)'
  - 'Tax Clearance/Official Receipt for Real Property Tax'
  - 'Occupancy Permit/Building Clearance'
  - 'MENRO Clearance'
  - 'Contract of Lease (if lease)'
  - 'Fire Safety Inspection Certificate'
  - 'SSS Certificate/Certificate of Compliance'
  - 'Employee list'
  - 'Two (2) Valid IDs: Birth certificate, NBI Clearance, Certificate of Employment, or Affiliation from the main company or distributor (for non-resident owners)'

  - 'Gross sales (1 printed copy)'
requirements_business_specific:
  - 'For Gas Stations: 1 original copy of Certificate of Compliance from Energy and Regulatory Board'
  - 'For LPG operators: 1 original copy of Certification of license to operate from Dept of Energy'
  - 'For businesses serving food: 1 original copy of laboratory test results of employees'
  - 'For gravel and sand/hollow blocks businesses: Certification from Source of Quarry Materials'
  - 'For money changers/remittance centers/pawnshops, etc.: Certificate of Registration from BSP'
  - 'For private schools: Permit/Recognition from the Department of Education'
requirements_optional:
  - 'Authorization letter from the owner/manager (if representative)'
