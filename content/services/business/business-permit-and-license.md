---
title: 'Barangay Clearance'
description: 'Secure an official Barangay Clearance for local transactions, building clearances, and employment.'
fees: '₱50.00'
time: '10-15 mins.'
office: 'Barangay Hall Desk 1 (BPLO Division)'
requirements:
  - 'Valid ID (with local address)'
  - 'Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)'
  - 'Recent Proof of Residency'
steps:
  - 'Present your requirements to the desk officer for verification.'
  - 'Wait for the encoder to process and print your digital clearance record.'
  - 'Proceed to the Treasury window to settle the processing fee of ₱50.00.'
  - 'Claim your signed Barangay Clearance with the municipal dry seal applied.'
---
