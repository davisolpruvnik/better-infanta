  - 'Filled out application form for business permit'
  - 'Location sketch/picture of the business location'
  - 'DTI registration certificate'
  - 'Barangay clearance'

  - Zoning certificate/locational clearance (does not apply to those in ICC/IMFP)
  - Tax Clearance/Official Receipt for Real Property Tax
  - Occupancy Permit/Building Clearance
  - MENRO Clearance
  - Contract of Lease (if lease)
  - Fire Safety Inspection Certificate
  - Employee list
  - Copy of Mayor's Permit issued by the LGU where the main business is located
  - Authorization letter from the owner/manager if representative
  - Two (2) Valid IDs Birth certificate/NBI Clearance/Certificate of Employment/Affiliation from the main company or distributor (for non-resident owners)
  - Optional requirements

  - Business owners or their representatives

  - 'Proceed to the Office of the Municipal Treasurer and write the information on a piece of paper provided by the assigned staff.'
  - 'Pay the corresponding amount.'
  - 'Sign and claim the CTC/cedula.'
