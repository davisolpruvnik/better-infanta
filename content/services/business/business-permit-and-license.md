title: "Business License and Mayor's Permit"
description: "This service provided under the Mayor's Office for legitimacy of business operations in Infanta."
fees: 'Varies'
fee_details: 'Fees depend on the amount of capitalization, nature of buiness, and/or gross income.'
time: '1 day 30 mins'
office: 'Business Permits & Licensing Office (BPLO)'
whocanavail:
  - 'Local business owners or representatices'
  - 'Sole Proprietorships, Partnerships, & Corporations or representatives'
requirements:
  - 'Filled out application form for business permit'
  - 'Location sketch/picture of the business location'
  - 'DTI registration certificate'
  - 'Barangay clearance'
  - 'Cedula/CTC'
  - Zoning certificate/locational clearance (does not apply to those in ICC/IMFP)
  - Tax Clearance/Official Receipt for Real Property Tax
  - Occupancy Permit/Building Clearance
  - MENRO Clearance
  - Contract of Lease (if lease)
  - Fire Safety Inspection Certificate
  - Employee list
requirements_conditional:
  - 'DTI Registration (for Sole Proprietorships)'
  - 'SEC Articles of Incorporation (for Corporations)'
  - Copy of Mayor's Permit issued by the LGU where the main business is located
requirements_optional:
  - "Previous Year's Mayor's Permit (for renewals)"
  - Authorization letter from the owner/manager if representative
  - Two (2) Valid IDs Birth certificate/NBI Clearance/Certificate of Employment/Affiliation from the main company or distributor (for non-resident owners)

steps:
  - 'Submit all requirements at the BPLO reception window.'
  - 'BPLO officers evaluate your documentation and verify tax maps.'
  - 'Wait for spatial inspections by the Engineering & Health team.'
  - "Settle BPLO fees at the Treasurer's cashier desk."
  - "Claim your laminated Mayor's Business Permit and official plate."
