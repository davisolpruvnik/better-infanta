description: 'This service provides procedures for issuing a document that contains the type / kind of fishing gear and its specifications being used by a fisher folk that is as stated in Philippine Fisheries Code of 1998 (or RA 8550).'
fees: '~P790*'
fee_details: 'The indicated fee does not include fees for fish pots (which is P25 per piece for big; P10 per piece for small).'
time: '11 minutes'
time_details:
office: 'Office of the Municipal Agriculturist: Crops/Fishery Section'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All marginal fisher folks who are residents of Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - 'One (1) original copy of duly accomplished Municipal Fishing Gear Registration Form (from the Office of the Municipal Agriculturist)'

steps:
  - "Proceed to Office of the Municipal Agriculturist (OMA) and submit duly accomplished Application Form"
  - "Proceed to Treasurer’s Office for payment. | The office is located near the BPLO at the Local Finance Hall."
  - "Return to OMA to receive the certificate."
