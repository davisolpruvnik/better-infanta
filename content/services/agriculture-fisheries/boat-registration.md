description: 'This service provides procedures for issuing a document showing proof of ownership of fishing vessel with no more than gross weight of 3 tons.'
fees: 'Varies*'
fee_details: 'Fees depend on weight of the boat: P100 for less than 1 ton, P150 for 1 - 1.99 tons, and P200 for 2 - 2.99 tons; documentary stamp costs P15.'
time: '18 minutes'
time_details:
office: 'Office of the Municipal Agriculturist: Crops/Fishery Section'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All fisherfolks of Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - 'Admeasured Municipal Fishing Boat, 3 Gross Tonnage (GT) and Below'
  - 'One (1) set original copy of filled out Boat Admeasurement Form'
  - 'One (1) photocopy of Barangay Clearance'
  - One (1) photocopy of Community Tax Certificate/Cedula
  - One (1) original copy of Police Clearance

steps:
  - "Proceed to Office of the Municipal Agriculturist (OMA) to accomplish and submit Boat Registration Form and requirements."
  - " Proceed to Municipal Treasurer’s Office (MTO) and pay the corresponding registration fee. | The office is located near the BPLO at the Local Finance Hall. Fees also depend on boat's tonnage: P100 for less than 1 ton, P150 for 1 - 1.99 tons, and P200 for 2 - 2.99 tons; documentary stamp costs P15."
  - "Return to OMA to present the official receipt from the Treasury Office and receive the certificate."
