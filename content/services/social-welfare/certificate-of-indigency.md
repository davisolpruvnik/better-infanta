---
title: 'Certificate of Indigency Issuance'
description: 'A certificate provided to an indigent client/applicant as a requirement in availing assistance to other offices/institutions.'
fees: 'Free of charge'
time: '22 mins'
office: 'Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office'
office_address: 'Ground Floor near Reception, Infanta Municipal Building'
requirements:
  - 'Barangay certificate that states an applicant as belonging to an idigent family in the said area.'
  - 'Certificate of No Property from the Municipal Assessor's Office'
  - 'Certificate of No Business from the Municipal Treasury'
  - 'Certificate of Tax Exemption from the BIR'
whocanavail:
  - Any individual (18 years old and above)
  - Any corporation/company
  - Associations or registered organized groups.
steps:
  - 'Submit the requirements to MSWDO and provide the necessary information during the interview.'
  - 'The concerned agency will assess the requirements and prepare certificate if requirements are complete.'
  - 'Claim the issued certificate.'
---
