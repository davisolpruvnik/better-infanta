---
title: 'Issuance of Persons with Disability ID (PWD ID)'
description: 'An identification card issued to persons with disability for use in availing benefits as a PWD in accordance to RA 9442.'
fees: 'Free of charge'
time: '50 mins'
office: 'Office of the Municipal Mayor - PDAO'
office_address: '2nd Floor beside the Council Hall, Infanta Municipal Hall, Brgy. Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - Duly accomplished PWD Application Form (from PDAO)
  - Medical Certificate indicating the disability, (secure from a licensed physician)
  - Barangay Certificate of Residency (get from the barangay where the applicant resides/lives)
  - 1x1 picture, 2 pieces
requirements_for_apparent_disability:
  - Whole body picture
whocanavail:
  - Persons with disabilities (PWDs) who are residents of the municipality of Infanta and have a permanent disability certified by a licensed physician
steps:
  - 'Fill-out the Philippine Registry Application Form (PRPWD) provided by the PDAO staff.'
  - 'Submit the duly accomplished form along with the complete requirements.'
  - "A PDAO staff or head will conduct an interview to verify the applicant's disability and residency."
  - '> For those with apparent disability, the staff/head will request permission to take a photo of the client for the preparation of the apparent disability certificate.'
  - '> For those without apparent disability, submit the medical certificate issued by a licensed physician.'
  - 'Wait for the approval of the ID card and its preparation together with the booklets. | The waiting time for the approval consists of the staff encoding data, assigning a PWD ID number, and preparing the cards and booklets.'
  - 'Claim the PWD ID card and the booklets. | The staff will encode the data to the national disability database to ensure your status gets recognized around the Philippines.'
---
