---
title: 'Death Certificate Registration and Issuance'
description: 'A certificate provided to an indigent client/applicant as a requirement in availing assistance to other offices/institutions.'
fees: '~P620'
fee_details:
- "Fixed P620 fee and the additional (but optional) death certificate copies; P40 for the first copy and P25 for the succeeding copies."
time: '30-55 minutes'
office: 'Local Civil Registrar'
requirements:
  - 'Barangay certificate on the circumstances surrounding the death (should be secured in the barangay hall of a barangay where the death took place)'
  - 'Indications if the death occurred at home or in the hospital'
  - 'Personal appearance of the informant, who must be the deceased's relative'
  - 'Four (4) death certificate copies prepared by the hospital'
whocanavail:
  - "A relative (spouse/child(ren)/relative) of the deceased or the nearest kin or barangay official in some cases."
steps:
  - 'If the death occurred at home, submit to civil registry office the Barangay certificate and the duly accomplished death certificate issued by the municipal health officer. If at the hospital, submit to civil registry office the four (4) copies of the hospital-issued death certificate and signed by the municipal health officer.'
  - 'Proceed to the municipal treasury office to pay the required fees.'
  - 'Return to the municipal civil registry office and present the original official receipt issued by the municipal treasurer's office.'
  - 'Claim the duly registered certificate.'
  - 'Optional: Request for a certified machine copy of the death certificate. There is a P40 fee for the first copy and P25 for each succeeding copies.'
---
