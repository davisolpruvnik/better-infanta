---
title: 'Death Certificate Registration and Issuance'
description: 'A certificate provided to an indigent client/applicant as a requirement in availing assistance to other offices/institutions.'
fees: 'Varies'
fee_details:
- "Fixed P40 fee for the first copy, P25 for additional (but optional) death certificate copies; payment to municipal treasurer's office depend on burial's location and computation, but a P60 burial fee if to be buried outside public cemetery."
time: '27-40 minutes'
office: 'Local Civil Registrar'
office_address: "Ground Floor, Left Wing, Infanta Municipal Hall, Brgy. Poblacion, Infanta, Quezon"
office_hours: "Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)"
requirements_died_at_home:
  - 'Barangay certificate on the circumstances surrounding the death (should be secured in the barangay hall of a barangay where the death took place)'
  - 'Personal appearance of the informant, who must be the deceased's relative'
requirements_died_at_hospital:
  - 'Four (4) death certificate copies prepared by the hospital where the death took place'
  - 'Official receipt of Burial Certificate Clearance from the Municipal Treasurer's Office'

whocanavail:
  - "A relative (spouse/child(ren)/relative) of the deceased or the nearest kin or barangay official in some cases."
steps:
  - 'Follow this step if the death occurred at home.'
  - > Submit to civil registry office the required documents (for at-home death) and the duly accomplished death certificate issued by the municipal health officer. | Additional process for delayed registrations: administer an Oath. The documents will be encoded and assigned of a registry number.
  - > Claim the duly registered death certificate.
  - >> (Optional) The applicant can also request a certified true copy of the death certificate, costing P40 for the first copy and P25 for succeeding copies.
  - >> The personnel from civil registry will issue an order of payment, to which the applicant must then proceed to the Municipal Treasurer's Office, in front of Windows 5 and 6 of BPLO, to settle the payment. | The MTO will issue an official receipt upon payment.
  - >> Return to the civil registry and present the official receipt given by the MTO and claim the certified tru copy(ies) of the death certificate.
  - 'Follow this step if the death occurred at the hospital.'
  - '> Submit to civil registry office the required documents (for hospital death) and the duly accomplished death certificate issued by the municipal health officer. | Additional process for delayed registrations: administer an Oath. The documents will be encoded and assigned of a registry number.'
  - '> Claim the duly registered death certificate.'
  
---
