---
title: 'Solo Parent Identification Card (Solo Parent ID)'
description: 'An identification card issued to a single parent as one of the requirements in availing assistance/benefits as a solo parent in accordance to RA 8972.'
fees: 'Free of charge'
time: '3 hrs 22 mins'
office: 'Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO)'
office_address: 'Ground Floor, near Reception Desk, Infanta Municipal Hall, Brgy. Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - 'Birth certificate of children (minor age) from the local civil registry or PSA'
  - 'Barangay certification of being a single parent'
  - 'Proof of being a solo parent from the local civil registry or PAO (e.g. death certificate of spouse, court order, etc.)'
  - 'Income tax return (ITR) from BIR'
whocanavail:
  - 'Solo parents'
steps:
  - 'Submit the requirements to MSWDO and provide the necessary information during the interview.'
  - 'The concerned agency will assess the requirements and conduct validation through home visitations and collateral investigations, then prepare the ID if the applicant satisfies in the procedures.'
  - 'Claim the issued ID card, have it validated and signed by the MSWD officer and signed by the local chief.'
---
