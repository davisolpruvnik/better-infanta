---
title: 'Issuance of ID Cards for Senior Citizens (Senior Citizen ID)'
description: 'An identification card and booklet issued to senior residents of Infanta, as one of the requirements in availing privileges/benefits as a senior citizen in accordance to RA 9994 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010).'
fees:
time: '12 mins'
office: 'Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA)'
office_address: 'Senior Citizens Building - OSCA, Government Service Complex, Barangay Comon, Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - 'Duly-accomplished Application Form, 1 original copy (from OSCA)'
  - '1x1 picture of the applicant'
  - 'Birth certificate, 1 photocopy (from the local civil registry)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All residents aged 60 and above (senior citizens) with at least 6 months of residency'
steps:
  - 'Proceed to the Office for Senior Citizens Affairs and accurately fill out the application form given by the OSCA personnel. | Have the form signed first by the President of the Barangay Elderly Association (BEA).'
  - 'Submit the duly accomplished application form together with the requirements.'
  - 'Claim the issued ID card and booklet.'
---
