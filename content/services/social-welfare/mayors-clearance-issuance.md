title: "Mayor's Clearance / Certification Issuance"
description: 'The Mayor’s Clearance is issued to bona fide residents of the municipality, certifying that they have no pending cases against them. Certifications are issued to attest to a person’s status or level of achievement and to affirm the validity of the information provided.'
fees: ₱90
fee_details:
time: '15 mins'
office: 'Office of the Municipal Mayor'
office_address: '2nd Floor beside the Council Hall, Infanta Municipal Hall, Brgy. Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'Legitimate residents of Infanta, Quezon'
requirements:
  - 'Police clearance'
  - 'Barangay clearance'
  - 'Community tax certificate/cedula'
  - 'Documentary stamp tax'

steps:
  - 'Step 1: Submit the requirements and state the purpose of request.'
  - '> Purpose of Request | The purpose can range from employment, application to civil services, marriage, and more.'
  - '> A personnel from the office of the mayor will check the requirements, interview the applicant, and assess the purpose of the clearance.'
  - '> Certificate Preparation | With the clearance prepared and printed, review the document for securing the signature of the mayor.'
  - 'Step 2: Proceed to the Office of the Municipal Treasury to pay the required fee. | The office is located beside Local Finance Hall and BPLO of Infanta Municipal Hall. Applicants shall pay ₱90 and must be given an order of payment and official receipt upon paying.'
  - 'Step 3: Releasing | Return to the office of the mayor to claim the signed clearance.'
postscripts:
  - "Always keep your Mayor's Permit laminated and displayed prominently at your business location."
