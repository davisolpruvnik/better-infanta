---
title: 'Issuance of Locational Clearance'
description: 'A document issued to individuals ensuring that the construction of house/building is compliant'
fees: 'Varies'
fee_details: 'Depending on the business.'
time: '1 hr 30 mins'
time_details:
office: 'Office of the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator/Zoning Administrator (Zoning Section)'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'Developers, house contractors, lot/building owners, business proprietors, private/public companies/agencies and stakeholders of development in Infanta, Quezon'
requirements_:
  - 'Duly accomplished application form for Locational Clearance'
  - "Vicinity map, drawn to any scale showing the following: exact location of the proposed site, contiguous firms and/or institution and their products and major activities, and approximate distance of contiguous firms and/or institution within 1,000 meter radius from the proposed site and from the main service road leading to it."
  - "Barangay certification of no objection to the proposed project"
  - "Cost estimates of the project"
  - 'Copy of BIR Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR) (from Bureau of Internal Revenue)'
  - 'Two Photocopies of Official Receipt of Issuance Fee (+ documentary stamp) and Official Receipt for Transfer Tax and Fees (from Municipal Treasurer’s Office)'
requirements_if_applicable:
  - 'Community Tax Certificate or Authorization/SPA'

requirements_if_agricultural_(additional):
  - "Secure approval of reclassification from the Office of the Sangguniang Bayan/conversion clearance from the Department of Agrarian Reform"
  
requirements_in_behalf_of_applicant_(additional):
  - "Authorization or SPA in behalf of the applicant"
  
requirements_specific_businesses_(additional):
  - "Site development plan signed by a licensed environmental planner (if the project is resort/gasoline station/ice plant/hospital/clinic/dialysis center/poultry farm/piggery farm/cemetery/ricemill, among others)"
  - "Building plan, drawn to any scale showing the following: Site areas and boundaries, number of storey/s of plan building/s and size of the area occupied, road system within site premises, topography plan (for mining/quarrying only), Lot and block number (if subdivision and other related housing project)"

steps:
  - "Sign-in in the office log book in the zoning (now BPOSS) receiving area."
  - "Submit the required documents for checking."
  - "Proceed to the MTO and pay the required fees and photocopy the official receipt. | See revenue code here."
  - "Return to the BPOSS receiving area and present/give the photocopy of the official receipt."
  - "Notarize the clearance form and return it to the office BPOSS receiving area. | Review and sign the locational clearance."
  - "Wait a call from the office (BPOSS) and receive the approved locational clearance. | The staff will upload the locational clearance to the e-BPLS; if the uploaded documents are complete, the locational clearance applied via e-BPLS will be approved; otherwise, it will be declined."
---
