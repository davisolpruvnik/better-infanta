---
title: 'Zoning Certification for Application of House/Building and Other Land Activities'
description: "One of the prerequisite documents before a person/applicant can get Locational Clearance, Quarry Permit, Land Use Reclassification, Preliminary Approval for Locational Clearance, DENR Certificate of Non-Coverage/Environmental Compliance Certificate and other related requirements from other agencies to ensure that the location of the proposed project is compliant with the Comprehensive Land Use Plan and Zoning Ordinance (CLUP/ZO)."
fees: 'P180'
fee_details: 'Fees include P150 and a DST of P30.'
time: '4 hr 50 mins'
time_details:
office: 'Office of the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator/Zoning Administrator (Zoning Section)'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All'
requirements_mandatory:
  - 'Duly accomplished application form for ZC '
  - 'Certification of land ownership, click the certificate of land ownership button for detailed applicable requirements (any of the requirements is enough, not necessarily all)'
requirements_certificate_of_land_ownership_(part_of_mandatory_requirements):
  - "Certificate of title and tax declaration registered in the name of the applicant;"
  - "Tax declaration, declared in the name of the applicant plus certification from the DENR-CENRO (indicated the name of the lawful claimant/tenured migrant);"
  - "Deed of sale/deed of donation/contract of lease and other claimed of ownership (kapahintulutan sa pagpapatayo ng bahay/gusali/pagpapatunay sa barangay) plus certification of title and tax declaration of the registered owner;"
  - "SPA or authorization from the registered owner allowing the applicant to use the subject parcel of land (notarized) plus certification of title and tax declaration of the registered owner"
requirements_if_applicable_(additional):
  - "Approved subdivision plan/site development plan of lot"
  - "DHSUD (former HLURB) certification"
  - "Certification from MENRO/DENR-MGB application for quarry permit"
  - "Notarized board resolutions/secretary’s Certificate (for corporation)"
  - "Sketch plan or layout with site areas and boundaries (if the project is less than 20 square meters and made from light materials)"
  - "Forest Land Use Agreement/Foreshore Lease Agreement"
  - "No pending case (for the lot)"
  - "Waiver of Rights"
  - "Waiver from DPWH"
  - "Certificate of Occupancy (COO)/Survey of Protected Area Occupants (SrPAO) with DENR Certification of tenured migrant from DENR-CENRO"
  - "Barangay Certification indicated the year of occupancy in the concerned barangay"
  - "PAMB Resolution/SAPA clearance and other DENR clearances for project located in the protected areas/highly critical areas"
  - Other supporting documents

steps:
  - "Sign-in in the office log book in the zoning receiving area, now Building Permit One-Stop Shop (BPOSS)."
  - "Submit the required documents for checking. | If the requirements are complete, an ocular inspection will be scheduled. If incomplete, the staff will return the documents to the applicant."
  - "Assist the zoning inspector in conducting ocular inspection/ mapping to locate the exact site of the project. | For projects like condominiums, subdivisions, memorial parks, cemeteries, etc., consider looking at the Revenue Code for corresponding fees or other matters beyond the P180 fee."
  - "Wait for a call from the BPOSS."
  - "Pay the required fee at the Treasurer’s Office by showing the order of payment and photocopy the official receipt."
  - "Return to the BPOSS receiving area and submit a photocopied official receipt."
  - "Do one of the processes below depending on the evaluation."
  - "> If application is denied, the agency will advise an applicant to appeal to the Local Zoning Board of Appeals (LZBA) for further review of the application."
  - "> If application is approved, wait for the release/issuance of the certification and sign in the releasing log book after securing the certification."
