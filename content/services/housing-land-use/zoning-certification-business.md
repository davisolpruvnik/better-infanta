---
title: 'Zoning Certification for Business Permit Application'
description: "One of the required documents before a person can get a business permit ensuring that the location of the business establishment is compliant based on Infanta's Comprehensive Land Use Plan and Zoning Ordinance (CLUP/ZO)."
fees: 'P105'
fee_details: 'Fees include P75 and a DST of P30.'
time: '1 hr 5 mins'
time_details:
office: 'Office of the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator/Zoning Administrator (Zoning Section)'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All business establishment owners'
requirements_for_new/renewal_applicants_with_promissory_note:
  - 'Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) certificate indicated the latest name and address of the business establishment'
  - 'Current pictures of frontage and interiors of the business establishment with boundaries'
  - 'Barangay certification with no objection of the business establishment'
requirements_additional_(if_needed):
  - "If the location is inside a subdivision/predominantly residential area, a promissory note/certificate of no objection from the homeowners' association or neighbor's consent; or,"
  - "Photocopy of issued zoning certification/locational clearance"

steps:
  - "Sign-in in the office log book in the BPLO receiving area."
  - "Submit the required documents for checking. | If the requirements are complete, the assigned staff will issue and upload the zoning certification for business permit application via the e-BPLS system. If incomplete, the staff will return the documents to the BPLO and/or decline and mark the applied certification with incomplete requirements if already uploaded by the BPLO Staff via e-BPLS."
  - "Proceed to MTO for payment and photocopy the official receipt. | The fee breakdown is at P75 plus a DST of P30, with a total of P105."
  - "Wait for the releasing of certification and sign-in in the releasing log book after securing the certification."
---
