---
title: 'Correction in Area/Boundaries'
description: "A document correcting the area/boundaries found on the tax declaration as a result of the approved survey plan. It contains the new area and the newly computed market value and assessed value."
fees: 'Varies'
fee_details: 'Real Property Tax; issuance fee of P90.'
time: 'Varies*'
time_details: "Processing time can vary, 40 minutes apply to lands with market values not exceeding P2 million. Those exceeding are forwarded to provincial assessor's office for approval of assessment, which can last between 10-15 days depending on the completeness/status of the documents."
office: 'Office of the Municipal Assessor'
office_address: '2/F Infanta Municipal Building, Poblacion 38, Infanta, Quezon'
office_hours: 'Mon to Fri, 8AM-5PM (No Noon Break)'
whocanavail:
  - 'All clients with title but the area/boundaries is/are different from tax declaration'
requirements_:
  - 'Two (2) Photocopies of Certified True Copy of Title (from the Registry of Deeds)'
  - Three (2) Photocopies of Approved Subdivision Plan (from Licensed Geodetic Engineer, Registry of Deeds, DENR Regional Office)
  - 'Two (2) Photocopies Official Tax Receipt for the Current Year (from Municipal Treasurer’s Office)'
  - 'Two Photocopies of Official Receipt of Issuance Fee (+ documentary stamp) (from Municipal Treasurer’s Office)'
requirements_if_the_applicant_is_not_the_property_owner:
  - 'Community Tax Certificate or Authorization/SPA'

steps:
  - "Submit the required documents and provide the necessary information during interview. | A site inspection may be conducted if and when the Municipal Assessor so requires."
  - "Proceed to MTO for payment. | Fees include: real property tax and a P90 issuance fee."
  - "Go back to Municipal Assessor’s Office and present the photocopies of Official Receipt together with the Community Tax Certificate. | If market value exceeds P2 million, documents are forwarded to the Provincial Assessor in Lucena for approval of assessment."
  - "Receive duly approved Tax Declaration and Notice of Assessment."

postscripts:
  - "--> An additional 1 hour is also added in some applications for site inspections if the Municipal Assessor requires."
